NewsBook

Regional newspapers, all in one place

NewsBook is a mobile app for discovering and reading newspapers curated for your state and district — pick a location, and read what matters locally.

About NewsBook

NewsBook helps readers find newspapers and news posts relevant to their own district, published directly by local newspaper administrators. No clutter, no irrelevant national noise by default — just the newspapers curated for where you live.

Pick your area

Select your state and district once, and NewsBook shows newspapers curated for that area.

Read local news

Browse posts published by the newspaper administrators for your selected region.

Built for newspapers

Local newspaper teams get their own admin tools to publish posts and ads for their readers.
